Past Art

Human artistic expression began over 40,000 years ago with Paleolithic cave paintings, evolving through distinct cultural and historical periods such as the Ancient World, Medieval, and Early Modern eras. Early art periods were not just decorative — they were visual expressions of the beliefs, daily life, and worldviews of the societies that created them. Each era’s works reflected the cultural, spiritual, and political priorities of the time.

Modern Art

Modern art is defined by a conscious rejection of traditional academic rules, classical realism, and linear perspective, focusing instead on innovation, personal expression, and how the modern world feels rather than how it looks
