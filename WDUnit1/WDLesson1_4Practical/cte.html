Computer Programming and Web Design

"

The Computer Programming and Web Design Career and Technical Education (CTE) program gives students hands-on training in software engineering, coding languages, and digital innovation. Through project-based learning and work experiences, students explore front-end and back-end development using tools like HTML, CSS, JavaScript, Python, and Java. The curriculum prepares learners for high-demand careers in web design, app building, and systems programming, while offering opportunities to earn industry-recognized credentials and build a strong foundation for higher education in computer science.
